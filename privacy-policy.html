Privacy Policy

App: Flash News Upload
Effective date: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Flash News Upload ("the App") is a personal automation tool that publishes videos created by the user to the user's own TikTok account via the official TikTok Content Posting API. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

2. Data We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account, the App receives the following data through TikTok's OAuth 2.0 authorization:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): your TikTok open_id, display name, and avatar.
  • Video files you choose to publish: video files and metadata (title, description, privacy settings) that you explicitly select for publishing.
  • OAuth tokens: access tokens and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, required to call the TikTok API on your behalf.

The App does not collect data from any other person. It operates only on the TikTok account you connect.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you with TikTok and obtain API access tokens.
  • To upload and publish the videos you select to your own TikTok account, with the privacy settings you choose.
  • To refresh expired access tokens so publishing continues to work.

Your data is never used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than publishing content you direct the App to publish.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens and account identifiers are stored locally on the user's own machine (for example, in a local configuration file). They are never transmitted to any third-party server operated by the App developer. Video files are processed locally and sent directly to TikTok's servers via the official API.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party, except as required to call TikTok's official APIs (which necessarily receive the content you choose to publish) or as required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Tokens are retained only for as long as you use the App. You may delete all locally stored data at any time by removing the App's configuration files from your machine. You may also revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
